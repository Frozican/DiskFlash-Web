A browser-based companion inspired by cflash-git. Prepare an image, review the write settings, and generate a clear flash plan.

!
Browser limitation

This web app cannot directly overwrite ordinary USB drives like Linux dd. It can inspect your image and prepare the workflow, but it will never pretend a drive was flashed.
